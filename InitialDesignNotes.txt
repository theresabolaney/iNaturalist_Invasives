Initial Design Notes

- What problem are we trying to solve?
- Why are we trying to solve this problem?
- Who are the users for this solution?
- At a high level, what does a solution look like?
- List research into creating the solution, including:
-- Possible benefits of any implementation
-- Technical constraints or drawbacks to a given implementation
-- Estimation of time and knowledge required for implementation
- Next steps once scope and initial design are investigated/decided
